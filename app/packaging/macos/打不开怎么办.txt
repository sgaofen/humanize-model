Humanizer 目前没有 Apple 开发者签名,第一次打开时 macOS 会拦一下。任选一种:

1) 把 Humanizer 拖进「应用程序」,双击一次(会提示无法验证开发者/无法确认没有恶意软件,点「完成」),
   然后打开「系统设置 → 隐私与安全性」,拉到最下面点「仍要打开」,再输入密码确认。
2) 或者在「终端」里执行一行(会去掉下载标记):
   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Humanizer.app

之后双击 Humanizer 就会在浏览器里打开。它没有窗口,也不在 Dock 里;退出在网页右上角「…」菜单里。

Humanizer isn't signed with an Apple Developer ID yet, so macOS blocks the first launch. Either:

1) Drag Humanizer to Applications and double-click it once (dismiss the warning), then open
   System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click "Open Anyway".
2) Or run this in Terminal:  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Humanizer.app

After that, double-clicking Humanizer opens it in your browser. It has no window and no Dock icon;
quit from the "…" menu at the top right of the page.
