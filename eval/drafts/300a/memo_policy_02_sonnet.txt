MEMORANDUM

TO: Executive Leadership Team
FROM: Regional Operations Planning
DATE: September 21, 2026
RE: Consolidation of Regional Sales Offices — Denver, Charlotte, Phoenix, and Columbus

**Recommendation**

We propose consolidating our four regional sales offices — Denver, CO; Charlotte, NC; Phoenix, AZ; and Columbus, OH — into two regional hubs, Denver and Charlotte, by the start of Q3 2025 (July 1, 2025). This restructuring is projected to save $1.8M annually through reduced lease obligations, consolidated facilities staff, and lower duplicate overhead across IT, admin, and office management functions.

**Financial Impact**

- Combined annual lease and facilities cost across all four sites: $4.6M
- Projected combined cost for two expanded hubs: $2.8M
- Net annual savings: $1.8M, beginning fiscal year 2026
- One-time transition costs (relocation, lease termination penalties, buildout): estimated $650K, to be absorbed in Q2–Q3 2025

**Timeline**

- October 2025: Finalize hub lease amendments and begin buildout at Denver (expanding from 12,000 to 22,000 sq ft) and Charlotte (from 10,000 to 20,000 sq ft)
- January–March 2025: Employee transition planning; relocation offers extended to Phoenix and Columbus staff
- April–June 2025: Phased office closures — Columbus closes April 30, 2025; Phoenix closes June 13, 2025
- July 1, 2025: Both hubs fully operational

**Workforce Considerations**

Of the 86 affected employees across Phoenix and Columbus, HR estimates 34 will accept relocation to Denver or Charlotte with standard relocation packages ($8,500 average per employee), 22 will transition to remote roles reporting to the nearest hub, and the remainder will be offered severance consistent with our existing reduction-in-force policy, administered through HR Business Partner Maria Delgado's team.

**Compliance**

Legal has confirmed WARN Act notice requirements apply to the Columbus office (32 employees); 60-day notices must be issued no later than March 3, 2025. Phoenix falls below the WARN threshold.

**Next Steps**

We request approval from the Executive Committee by October 15, 2025, to begin lease negotiations and initiate the required WARN notifications on schedule.
