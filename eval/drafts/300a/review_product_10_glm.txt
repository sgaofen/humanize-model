We stayed at the Ocean Coral & Turquesa beachfront property in Cancún for three nights, March 14–17, 2024, and honestly it was a mixed bag.

Let's start with the good: the location is unbeatable. Our room on the sixth floor had a stunning view of the turquoise water, and we fell asleep to the sound of waves every night. The beach itself was clean, with plenty of palapas and loungers even when we came down after 10 a.m. The pool area was beautiful too, though the swim-up bar got crowded by noon.

The staff were genuinely lovely. Ricardo at the front desk checked us in quickly and gave us a great tip about walking to Playa Delfines, about 15 minutes south, which ended up being the highlight of our trip. Breakfast at the Las Olas buffet was solid — fresh fruit, made-to-order omelets, and the best chilaquiles I've had outside Mexico City.

Now the complaints. The Wi-Fi was painfully slow, especially in the rooms. I had to finish some work emails on our second day and it took almost 40 minutes to upload a handful of documents. Speed tests showed 2–3 Mbps in the room; the lobby was better, maybe 10 Mbps, but who wants to sit in the lobby at 11 p.m.? For a hotel that advertises itself as business-friendly, this needs fixing.

Other minor gripes: the air conditioning in our room rattled all night, and the elevator nearest our wing was out of service for two of our three days, meaning a long walk around the building with our suitcases.

Dinner at the à la carte Italian restaurant required a reservation we couldn't get until our last night, which was frustrating.

Would we return? Probably, for the beach alone — but bring a book, and don't count on streaming anything. Three stars: great location and people, held back by tired infrastructure.
