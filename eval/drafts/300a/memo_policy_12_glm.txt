**INTERNAL MEMORANDUM**

**To:** Executive Leadership Team and Board Audit & Risk Committee
**From:** Dana Whitfield, Chief Compliance Officer
**Date:** June 14, 2025
**Re:** Q2 2025 AML Training Compliance Report and Remediation Plan

**Summary**

As of June 10, 2025, 94% of employees (1,872 of 1,991) have completed the mandatory annual Anti-Money Laundering training. While this exceeds our 90% internal target, 119 employees remain outstanding. A remediation plan is now underway to reach full compliance by July 31, 2025.

**Completion Breakdown**

- Corporate & Shared Services: 98% (outstanding: 6)
- Retail Banking Operations: 96% (outstanding: 41)
- Commercial Lending: 93% (outstanding: 22)
- Wealth Management: 89% (outstanding: 38)
- International (Singapore and London offices): 91% (outstanding: 12)

All 63 licensed relationship managers in Wealth Management completed the enhanced KYC module; the gap there is concentrated in support staff.

**Key Findings**

The primary drivers of non-completion are (1) extended medical or parental leave (27 employees) and (2) delayed onboarding for Q2 hires (44 employees). Only 48 employees have no documented reason for non-completion. The Singapore office lag stems from a scheduling conflict with the May system migration; make-up sessions were held June 3–5.

**Remediation Plan**

1. **June 16–20:** Direct supervisors of the 48 unexplained non-completers issue written notices; employees enrolled in mandatory live sessions on June 24 and July 8.
2. **June 30:** Leave-returnees auto-enrolled in training within five business days of return; HR to verify via payroll reactivation flags.
3. **July 15:** Q2 hires to complete onboarding curriculum; completion now a condition of probationary review.
4. **July 31:** Any remaining non-completers escalated to division heads; access to customer-facing systems suspended per Section 4.3 of the AML Policy.
5. **August 5:** Final attestation report to the Board Audit & Risk Committee.

**Regulatory Context**

Our regulator requires annual AML refresher training for all staff. Last year's examination noted a 91% completion rate as satisfactory but recommended escalation procedures for laggards — procedures now embedded in this plan.

**Budget Impact**

Remediation costs are estimated at $18,500 (live session facilitation and LMS licensing), absorbed within the existing FY2025 compliance training budget of $420,000.

No further action is requested from leadership at this time; the next status update will follow on August 5.
