During my six-week internship at Halden & Price Marketing, from August 11 through September 19, 2026, I supported the account team in developing campaign concepts, conducting market research, and preparing client-ready materials. I worked primarily from the firm’s San Francisco office with account manager Priya Shah and creative director Marcus Lee.

My main responsibility was assisting with the launch planning for Redwood Coffee Roasters, a regional client expanding into Sacramento and Oakland. I reviewed customer survey results from 428 respondents, analyzed competitor promotions across 12 local coffee brands, and summarized engagement data from Redwood’s Instagram and email channels. These findings helped the team identify students, remote workers, and commuters as the campaign’s strongest target segments.

I delivered three campaign drafts during the internship. The first, “Your Morning, Reimagined,” focused on short-form video and commuter discounts. The second, “Brew Local,” emphasized Redwood’s partnerships with Northern California suppliers and included a proposed $18,000 social media budget. The third combined loyalty rewards with location-based advertising and included sample posts, email copy, a six-week content calendar, and draft performance metrics. Each draft was reviewed during a weekly Thursday meeting, and I incorporated feedback from the creative, analytics, and client-services teams.

In addition to campaign development, I attended two client calls, prepared meeting notes, and updated the team’s project tracker in Asana. My work contributed to the final presentation scheduled for September 24, and the client selected elements from all three drafts for further testing. This internship strengthened my skills in marketing research, copywriting, presentation design, and collaborative project management.
