**Fundraising Report: Spring Gala 2025**

To: Riverside Community Club Board of Directors
From: Maria Delgado, Fundraising Chair
Date: April 14, 2025
Re: Spring Gala Results — Final Report

**Summary**

I'm pleased to report that the Spring Gala held on Saturday, April 5, at the Grandview Ballroom exceeded our fundraising goal, netting $18,750 against our target of $15,000 — a 125% achievement rate.

**Ticket Sales**

We sold 210 tickets at $75 each, generating $15,750 in ticket revenue. This surpassed our projection of 190 tickets and represented a 12% increase over last year's attendance of 188 guests. Walk-in sales on the night of the event accounted for 18 tickets, thanks to our social media push in the final week.

**Revenue Breakdown**

- Ticket sales: $15,750
- Silent auction: $2,300
- Live auction (5 items): $1,900
- Raffle and wine pull: $850
- Total gross: $20,800

**Expenses**

Total expenses came to $2,050, including catering ($1,400), rentals ($350), and decorations ($300). Our thanks to Vista Catering for discounting their services, and to committee members who donated all raffle and wine pull items. Net proceeds: $18,750.

**Highlights and Acknowledgments**

The live auction, emceed by James Okafor, was our strongest revenue segment per item, with the Lakeside Cabin weekend package alone bringing in $700. The silent auction featured 32 donated items from local businesses — a record for our club. Special thanks to the volunteer team of 14 members who handled setup, check-in, and checkout.

**Recommendations**

1. Increase the ticket price to $85 next year, given strong demand and a sellout by April 1.
2. Expand the live auction to 8 items based on this year's performance.
3. Book the Grandview Ballroom by September, as 2026 spring dates are filling quickly.

Funds will be allocated per board direction, with the prior plan directing proceeds to the Youth Scholarship Program and clubhouse roof repairs.

Please contact me with any questions about the attached financial detail.
