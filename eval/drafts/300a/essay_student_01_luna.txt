In Act 5, Scene 1 of William Shakespeare’s *Macbeth*, Lady Macbeth’s guilt appears through her sleepwalking, repeated hand-washing, and fragmented speech. Earlier in the play, she seemed confident and ruthless, urging Macbeth to murder King Duncan at Inverness after the feast in Act 1. However, by Act 5, her conscience has overwhelmed her. The Doctor and Gentlewoman observe her entering with a candle, suggesting that Lady Macbeth is afraid of the darkness and haunted by the crimes she helped create. Although she is asleep, she speaks as if she is reliving the murders of Duncan, Banquo, and Lady Macduff. Her words, “Out, damned spot!” reveal that she imagines blood on her hands. The “spot” is physically invisible, but psychologically it represents her permanent guilt over Duncan’s murder. Earlier, Lady Macbeth had claimed that “a little water clears us of this deed,” but her behavior in Scene 1 proves that guilt cannot be removed as easily as blood from the skin.

Her speech also shows that she remembers the details of the crimes. She says, “Who would have thought the old man to have had so much blood in him?” referring to Duncan, whose murder she once treated as a practical problem. She also cries, “The Thane of Fife had a wife; where is she now?” recalling Lady Macduff’s death at Macbeth’s command. These memories demonstrate that Lady Macbeth’s guilt has expanded beyond her own actions to include the violence of Macbeth’s reign. The Gentlewoman explains that Lady Macbeth has been washing her hands for fifteen minutes, emphasizing how deeply her guilt has affected her mind and body. Unlike Macbeth, who responds to fear by ordering more murders, Lady Macbeth is destroyed by the consequences of their ambition. Her guilt isolates her from the court and even from Macbeth, who is fighting the approaching English army led by Malcolm near Dunsinane Castle. Shakespeare therefore presents Lady Macbeth’s guilt as an unavoidable moral punishment: although she once believed herself stronger than conscience, the bloodshed she encouraged has permanently damaged her mind.
