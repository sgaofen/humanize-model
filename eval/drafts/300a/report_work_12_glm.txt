**Meeting Minutes Summary — Rotary Club Chapter 12**

**Date:** March 3
**Location:** Community Hall, Riverside Room
**Attendance:** 18 members; President Dana Whitfield presiding; Secretary Miguel Torres recording.

**Call to Order**
President Whitfield called the meeting to order at 12:15 p.m. The Pledge of Allegiance was led by Sergeant-at-Arms Ruth Okafor. Secretary Torres noted that the minutes of the February 24 meeting were approved as circulated without amendment.

**New Member Inductions**
The membership committee, chaired by Helen Park, presented two candidates for induction. Members voted unanimously by show of hands to approve:

- **Priya Raman**, pharmacist at Lakeside Health Center, sponsored by Helen Park
- **Daniel Osei**, owner of Osei Automotive on Maple Street, sponsored by Ruth Okafor

Both new members were welcomed and will receive their membership pins at the March 17 meeting.

**Committee Reports**

- **Treasurer:** Frank Delgado reported a current balance of $8,420. The annual pancake breakfast fundraiser grossed $3,150 against expenses of $900. The report was accepted as presented.
- **Service Projects:** Helen Park announced that the weekend literacy drive collected 412 children's books for Lincoln Elementary School. Volunteers are needed for sorting on March 8, 9:00 a.m. at the chapter storage unit.
- **Youth Programs:** Miguel Torres reported that scholarship applications are due April 1; six applications have been received to date.

**Old Business**
Discussion continued on the highway cleanup scheduled for April 12. Ruth Okafor confirmed the county will supply vests and trash bags. A sign-up sheet circulated; nine members have committed so far.

**New Business**
President Whitfield proposed forming an ad hoc committee to plan the annual awards banquet in June. Helen Park, Frank Delgado, and Daniel Osei volunteered. The committee will present a budget at the April 7 meeting.

**Announcements**
- District conference registration closes March 20; three members have registered.
- Next regular meeting: March 17, 12:00 p.m., Riverside Room.

**Adjournment**
The meeting adjourned at 1:20 p.m.

Minutes respectfully submitted by Miguel Torres, Secretary.
