200 hours in and I finally feel qualified to write this. Short version: Baldur's Gate 3 is one of the best RPGs I've ever played, and also occasionally one of the most frustrating.

Let's get the bugs out of the way first, because there are plenty. In my honor mode run (RIP at 140 hours to a Shadowheart pathing error that walked her off a cliff in the Shar temple), I've seen it all: characters teleporting through walls in the Grymforge, Karlach's dialogue cutting out mid-sentence, the infamous Act 3 performance dips in Lower City where my 3080 ti drops to 25 fps, and one memorable instance where Lae'zel refused to follow me for an entire session and I had to fast travel to unstick her. Patch 5 fixed a lot, but even now, six months after the August 2023 launch, I still hit the occasional broken quest flag. Last week Dame Aylin got stuck hovering outside my camp for three in-game days.

Here's the thing though: none of it stopped me from starting a fourth playthrough. The replay value is genuinely absurd. My first run was a goody-two-shoes Paladin, second was a Dark Urge bard who romanced Astarion (10/10, no notes), third was an evil drow run that completely changed Act 2. The origin characters are so well written that I'm now doing a Karlach origin run just to see her perspective. Act 3 alone has enough content that I found entire quest lines I'd missed on runs one and two — the circus of Last Light, the House of Hope, Mizora's whole arc.

Is it perfect? No. Act 3 is still jankier than Acts 1 and 2, and the epilogue slides feel rushed compared to everything else. But 200 hours across four characters and I'm still finding new dialogue, new outcomes, new ways to solve problems. The day I rolled a nat 20 to talk down a boss instead of fighting it, I understood what Larian built here.

Recommended. Obviously. Just save often.
