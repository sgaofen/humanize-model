Came here for a Friday-night birthday dinner and left seriously impressed, minor gripes aside. Obangdo Korean BBQ opened up on Burnet Road near Anderson Lane a few months back, and it's already doing better banchan than places that have been around for a decade. We got 9 side dishes with our order for two — the japchae, braised potatoes, and that cucumber kimchi were standouts, and they refilled the perilla leaves and pickled radish without us even asking twice.

The main event held up too: we did the "Obangdo Special" combo ($68) with marinated galbi, brisket, and pork belly, cooked over actual charcoal, not the gas inserts a lot of newer spots use. Our server Min came by every few minutes to flip and cut everything, which I appreciate since I always overcook it myself at home.

The catch is the wait. We showed up at 7:15 on a Saturday without a reservation and didn't get seated until 8:45 — an hour and a half standing around the tiny entryway with maybe six other parties. They don't take call-ahead waitlist yet, which feels like an easy fix for a place clearly popular enough to need one. The hostess was apologetic and comped us a round of soju for the wait, which softened the blow.

Food's a solid 5 stars. Experience overall lands at 4 because of that wait. Go on a weeknight if you can, or brace yourself for a long stand outside on weekends. Total for two with drinks and tip came to about $110.
