1/ Last month, my Etsy shop did $4,200 in revenue (August 1–31). After Etsy fees, shipping, materials, and ads, I kept about $2,180—not $4,200 profit. Here’s what actually sold:

2/ Best seller: personalized walnut recipe boards — 38 orders, $1,710 revenue. Most buyers were ordering wedding gifts, and the phrase “Grandma’s Kitchen” outsold every other customization.

3/ Second: birth flower art prints — 64 orders, $960 revenue. These were inexpensive to make and had the best margin, especially the digital downloads. August’s top combination was May + September flowers.

4/ Third: engraved dog tags — 29 orders, $725 revenue. “Luna,” “Charlie,” and “Milo” were the most common names. My $3 personalization upgrade converted surprisingly well.

5/ Everything else—stickers, keychains, and a new mug design—brought in $805 combined. The mugs got plenty of favorites but only 6 sales, so I’m pausing that line for now.

6/ What drove sales: Pinterest brought 41% of traffic, Etsy search brought 37%, and Instagram brought just 8%. My biggest day was August 12 after a Pinterest pin took off.

7/ Biggest lesson: the products with the strongest emotional angle—family, pets, milestones—won. I’m doubling down on personalized gifts for September and cutting anything people only “liked” but didn’t buy.
