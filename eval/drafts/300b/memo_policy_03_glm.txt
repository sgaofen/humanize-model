**MEMORANDUM**

**To:** All Employees
**From:** Dana Whitfield, VP of Human Resources
**Date:** November 14, 2024
**Re:** Expansion of Parental Leave Policy — Effective January 1, 2025

I am pleased to announce that, following a competitive benefits benchmarking review conducted this fall, we are expanding our paid parental leave benefit from 8 weeks to 16 weeks, effective January 1, 2025.

**Background**

In Q3, HR completed a benchmarking study of parental leave policies at 12 peer companies in our industry, including our three closest competitors for talent. The median paid parental leave among this group was 14 weeks, and several firms had recently moved to 16 weeks or more. Our previous 8-week benefit placed us in the bottom quartile and was cited in 23% of declined offer acceptances over the past 18 months, according to recruiting exit surveys.

**What's Changing**

Effective January 1, 2025, all eligible full-time employees who become parents through birth, adoption, or foster placement will receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave, up from 8 weeks. Key provisions:

- Leave must be taken within 12 months of the child's arrival
- Available to all parents regardless of gender or caregiving role
- May be taken continuously or in two blocks, with manager approval
- Employees whose leave began on or after October 1, 2024, may elect to extend their leave under the new policy by contacting HR

Employees remain eligible for job protection under the FMLA and applicable state laws; our policy now exceeds both.

**What's Not Changing**

Short-term disability benefits for birth mothers (up to 8 weeks, administered through Lincoln Financial) remain separate and run concurrently with the first weeks of parental leave. Our existing four weeks of paid caregiver leave for family medical emergencies is unchanged.

**Budget Impact**

The expansion carries an estimated annual cost of $740,000, approved by the Finance Committee on November 6 as part of the FY2025 benefits budget. We project this will be partially offset by reduced turnover; replacement costs for departing employees averaged 42% of salary in our 2023 analysis.

Questions can be directed to hr@company.com or raised at the December 3 all-hands meeting. A revised policy document will be posted to the HR portal by December 15.
