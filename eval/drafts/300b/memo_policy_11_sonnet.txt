TO: Executive Leadership Team
FROM: Human Resources — Total Rewards
DATE: September 21, 2026
RE: Phased Retirement Program for Employees 60+ (Voluntary, Pre-December 2026)

We propose launching a voluntary Phased Retirement Program on November 1, 2026, targeting 85 currently eligible employees aged 60 and older, with full transition completed by December 31, 2026.

**Rationale:** Workforce analysis identifies 85 employees across Manufacturing (34), Finance & Accounting (19), IT (17), and Corporate Administration (15) at our Dayton, Ohio and Charlotte, North Carolina sites who meet eligibility (age 60+, minimum 10 years of service). Retaining institutional knowledge while managing succession risk and payroll costs is the primary driver.

**Structure:** Eligible employees may elect a 12-month glide path: 80% schedule/80% pay for months 1–4, 60%/60% for months 5–8, and 40%/40% for months 9–12, retaining full health benefits throughout and continued 401(k) match on actual earnings. Participants formally separate on the earlier of month 12 or their chosen retirement date.

**Financial impact:** Estimated net payroll savings of $2.1M in FY2027, offset by a one-time knowledge-transfer/mentoring stipend of $180,000 ($2,100 per enrolled participant, capped at 60 participants). We project 60–65 of the 85 eligible employees will enroll based on comparable programs at peer firms.

**Compliance:** Participation is strictly voluntary and opt-in to comply with the Older Workers Benefit Protection Act (OWBPA) and ADEA. No employee may be pressured or defaulted into enrollment, and all communications will be reviewed by Legal (contact: General Counsel's office) before distribution. A 45-day consideration period and 7-day revocation window will apply to any release of claims signed in connection with enrollment.

**Timeline:**
- October 5, 2026: Legal review complete
- October 19, 2026: Manager briefings
- November 1, 2026: Enrollment window opens (closes November 30, 2026)
- December 2026: Transition begins for elected participants

We request approval to proceed with Legal review by September 30, 2026.
