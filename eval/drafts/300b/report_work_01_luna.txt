For the first quarter of 2026, Riverside Hiking Club maintained a closing balance of $4,230 as of March 31. The club began the quarter with $4,910 and spent $680 on trail maintenance between January 1 and March 31.

The maintenance expenses covered replacement tools, gravel, lumber, signage materials, and disposal fees for work completed along the Riverside Loop and Cedar Ridge trails. Volunteers completed two organized maintenance days on January 18 and March 14, clearing fallen branches, repairing drainage channels, repainting trail markers, and replacing three damaged directional signs.

All Q1 expenses were paid from the club’s operating account and supported by receipts submitted to the treasurer. No funds were used for personal purchases, and the club has no outstanding maintenance invoices as of March 31.

The remaining $4,230 includes funds reserved for spring safety improvements, insurance, permit fees, and upcoming member activities. Based on current expenses and expected dues, the club is in a stable financial position. The executive committee should continue prioritizing essential trail repairs while maintaining a reserve for emergency work after severe weather.
