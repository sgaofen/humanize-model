**TREASURER'S REPORT — Budget Variance Summary**
Q3 FY2025 (July 1 – September 30)
Prepared by: Dana Whitfield, Club Treasurer
Date: October 4, 2025

**Overview**

This report summarizes the variance between approved budget and actual expenditures for the third quarter, with emphasis on the Facilities category, which is currently over budget by $12,500.

**Budget vs. Actual — Facilities**

| Line Item | Budgeted (Q3) | Actual (Q3) | Variance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Routine maintenance | $4,000 | $4,150 | –$150 |
| HVAC | $2,500 | $15,800 | –$13,300 |
| Utilities | $6,000 | $5,930 | +$70 |
| **Total** | **$12,500** | **$25,880** | **–$12,500 (over)** |

**Explanation of Variance**

On August 12, 2025, the main HVAC unit serving the west wing (installed 2008) failed during a heat wave. Repairs performed by Caldwell Mechanical of Springfield included compressor replacement, coil repair, and refrigerant recharge. Total invoice: $13,300, received September 18. This single event accounts for the entire overage.

**Budget Adjustments**

To absorb the $12,500 overrun, the Executive Board approved the following reallocations on October 2:

- Defer purchase of two new conference tables ($4,200) to FY2026
- Reduce Q4 hospitality/events budget by $3,800
- Apply $4,500 from the Capital Reserve Fund, per the reserve policy allowing withdrawal for emergency repairs exceeding $5,000

These adjustments fully offset the variance without impacting member programs.

**Recommendations**

1. Establish a Facilities Contingency line of $8,000 in the FY2026 budget.
2. Schedule preventive HVAC maintenance twice yearly with Caldwell Mechanical under a service contract (estimated $1,800/year).
3. Obtain a second quote for any future repair exceeding $5,000.

**Overall Financial Position**

Total Q3 expenditures across all categories: $87,400 against a budget of $89,900, leaving us $2,500 under budget overall. Cash on hand as of September 30: $42,600.
