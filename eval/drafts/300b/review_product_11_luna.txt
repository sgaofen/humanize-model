I usually avoid roguelikes because repeating the same levels after dying feels more frustrating than fun, but *Hades* completely changed my mind. I started playing on Nintendo Switch in March 2024, expecting to quit after a few failed attempts. Instead, I spent roughly 35 hours trying to escape the Underworld as Zagreus.

The combat is fast but easy to understand: dash, attack, cast, and build a different combination of boons each run. My favorite moments came from choosing upgrades from Athena, Artemis, and Dionysus and seeing an ordinary sword turn into something surprisingly powerful. Even when I died in Tartarus or Elysium, I usually unlocked a new weapon, conversation, ability, or story detail back at the House of Hades, so progress never felt completely wasted.

The characters are the biggest surprise. Zagreus, Hades, Nyx, Achilles, and Megaera all have memorable personalities, and their dialogue changes constantly. The voice acting and soundtrack also make the repeated runs feel like part of an evolving adventure rather than grinding.

On Switch, the game runs smoothly in handheld mode, and each escape attempt is convenient to play in 20- to 40-minute sessions. Some late-game fights became hectic on the smaller screen, but I never had serious performance problems.

I still wouldn’t call myself a roguelike convert, but *Hades* is accessible, stylish, and generous enough that even a casual player can enjoy it. It’s one of the best games I’ve played on my Switch.
