We recommend closing the Centerville retail location when its lease expires on August 31, 2025. The store has remained unprofitable despite sustained management attention, cost controls, and efforts to improve sales. Its operating losses are diverting resources from higher-performing locations and limiting our ability to invest in e-commerce, customer retention, and profitable markets.

During the past 12 months, Centerville generated approximately $1.2 million in sales but incurred operating expenses of roughly $1.45 million, resulting in an estimated annual loss of $250,000. The location’s average monthly sales have declined by approximately 8% year over year, while rent, staffing, utilities, and inventory carrying costs have continued to increase. Store traffic is also substantially below the level required to support the current footprint.

Closing the location at lease expiration will avoid renewal obligations, reduce fixed operating costs, and eliminate the need for approximately $150,000 in planned facility and merchandising expenditures. We estimate that the closure will improve annual operating income by approximately $230,000 after accounting for one-time costs, including severance, inventory markdowns, relocation, and lease-exit activities.

Employees at the Centerville store should receive at least 60 days’ notice, with qualified team members offered transfers to the Riverside and Northgate locations where openings exist. Inventory should be redistributed to nearby stores and the company’s online channel beginning in June 2025, followed by a controlled clearance program in July and August. Customer communications should emphasize continued service through our online store and neighboring locations.

Management should provide formal notice to the landlord no later than May 31, 2025, complete employee consultations by June 15, and close the store after business on August 31, 2025. This action is financially prudent, operationally manageable, and consistent with the company’s strategy of concentrating resources on profitable channels and locations.
