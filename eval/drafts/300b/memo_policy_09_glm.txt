**MEMORANDUM**

**To:** All Department Heads and Regional Managers
**From:** Priya Nair, Director of Human Resources
**Date:** April 14, 2025
**Re:** Remote Work Eligibility Revisions — Results of the March Staff Survey

**Purpose**

This memo summarizes findings from the March 2025 remote work survey (1,240 respondents, an 82% response rate) and announces revised eligibility criteria effective June 2, 2025.

**Key Survey Findings**

- 74% of respondents support a hybrid model; only 11% prefer full-time office attendance.
- Productivity self-ratings were equal or higher for remote days in 87% of responses.
- Departments with fixed in-office mandates (Facilities, Front Desk Services, Print Production) reported the lowest satisfaction scores.
- 63% of staff cited commuting costs as a significant concern, averaging $340/month.

**Revised Policy, Effective June 2, 2025**

1. **Standard hybrid schedule:** Eligible employees may work remotely up to three days per week, increased from two, with manager approval.
2. **Fully remote classification:** Roles designated as location-independent (currently 112 positions across IT, Finance, and Marketing) may be performed fully remotely, subject to quarterly performance reviews.
3. **In-office essential roles:** Positions requiring physical presence — building security, mailroom, lab technicians at the Portland and Denver sites — remain onsite, with a commuting stipend of $75/month.
4. **Eligibility threshold:** Employees must have completed six months of service (previously twelve) and maintain a performance rating of "Meets Expectations" or higher.

**Implementation Requirements**

- Managers must submit updated team hybrid agreements to HR by May 16, 2025.
- All staff must complete the revised Remote Work Agreement in Workday by May 30, 2025.
- Remote employees within 50 miles of the Denver, Austin, or Boston offices must attend onsite once per month for team collaboration days.

**Compliance Notes**

Remote arrangements remain contingent on state tax and labor law compliance in the employee's work location. Requests to relocate out of state require 60 days' advance notice and HR Legal review.

Questions should be directed to hr-policy@company.com or raised at the April 24 all-hands briefing.
