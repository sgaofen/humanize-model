In January 2026, 38 members of our club volunteered a combined 412 hours at the Downtown Food Bank. Volunteers supported weekday evening and Saturday shifts at the food bank’s Main Street facility, assisting with food sorting, inventory organization, grocery-box assembly, and distribution to local families.

Participation remained steady throughout the month, with members contributing across all four weeks. The largest volunteer turnout occurred during the January 17–18 weekend, when members helped process incoming donations and prepare emergency food packages. Several volunteers also assisted staff with reorganizing the canned-goods storage area and labeling shelves to improve access to frequently requested items.

The 412-hour total represents an average of approximately 10.8 hours per participating member. Members worked alongside Downtown Food Bank staff and other community volunteers to sort donated produce, canned goods, grains, and household staples. Their efforts helped the organization maintain regular distribution services during a period of high winter demand.

We appreciate everyone who contributed time, transportation, and supplies to the January service effort. Special recognition goes to members who completed multiple shifts and helped coordinate sign-ups, reminders, and transportation to the Downtown Food Bank. Based on the success of this project, the club plans to continue supporting the food bank through monthly volunteer shifts and donation drives.
