I've been coming to Corner Cuts Barbershop on Maple Ave for about two years now, and I finally found my forever barber: Marcus. I booked with him for the first time back in March after my usual guy moved out of state, and I honestly should have switched to him sooner.

I asked for a mid fade with some texture on top, showed him a couple of reference photos, and he actually took the time to talk through what would work with my hair type (thick, cowlick right in the front — the bane of my existence). He explained how he'd blend the fade around the cowlick so it wouldn't stick up weird after a few days of growth. No other barber has ever done that.

The fade itself was flawless. The blend from the skin up through the guard lengths was seamless — no lines, no patchy spots, and perfectly symmetrical around the ears, which is where most barbers mess up. He also lined up my beard and matched it to the fade, which brought the whole look together. He didn't rush, spent about 45 minutes total, and checked in with me at each stage instead of just doing his own thing.

Shop itself is clean, good music (classic R&B the day I went), and they run on time — my 2:00 appointment on a Saturday actually started at 2:00, which is basically a miracle for a barbershop. Cash and card both accepted, $35 for the cut, well worth it.

Marcus is friendly without being overly chatty, which I appreciate. He remembered details about my job when I came back for my second appointment in April, which is a nice touch.

Ask for Marcus and book ahead — his Saturday slots fill up fast. I've already got my next appointment for the 15th.
