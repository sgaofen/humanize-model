As of March 24, 2026, 120 employees at Northstar Community Services have completed the mandatory GDPR compliance training module. The completed participants represent all departments, including Operations, Client Services, Finance, Human Resources, and the Manchester and Leeds offices.

The module covered the principles of data minimisation, lawful processing, individual data rights, secure handling of personal information, breach reporting, and procedures for responding to customer access requests. Completion records have been verified through the company’s learning management system, and certificates have been issued to all staff who finished the assessment.

Fifteen employees remain outstanding. Their managers have been notified and asked to ensure completion by March 31, 2026. The remaining employees are distributed across Client Services (six), Operations (four), Finance (three), and Human Resources (two). Reminder emails will be sent on March 26 and March 30, and department supervisors will receive an updated completion list each afternoon during the final week.

Priya Shah, Data Protection Officer, will review the final completion report on April 1 and follow up directly with anyone who has not completed the module. Employees who miss the deadline may be required to attend a scheduled catch-up session and could temporarily lose access to systems containing personal data until training is complete.

Overall progress is on track, with 88.9% of staff already compliant. The training initiative remains on schedule for full completion by March 31.
