During the week ending September 18, 2026, Kestrel Packaging delivered 96% of scheduled orders on time to our Oakland distribution center. This represents a slight improvement from the previous week’s 94% performance and remains above the 95% service-level target in our supplier agreement.

The supplier completed 48 of 50 scheduled shipments within the agreed delivery window. The two late shipments, both for 12-ounce food containers, arrived one and two business days behind schedule because of a carrier capacity issue in Reno, Nevada. Kestrel notified our procurement team on September 14 and provided revised delivery estimates before the shipments missed their appointments.

Two quality holds were issued during receiving inspection. The first involved 18 cartons of 16-ounce clamshell containers with warped lids; the second involved 11 cartons with inconsistent label placement that interfered with barcode scanning. Both lots were segregated at the Oakland facility on September 16. Kestrel Packaging acknowledged the findings and arranged replacement product at no additional cost. Corrective-action documentation is due from Kestrel by September 23.

Overall supplier performance is stable and currently meets the contracted delivery expectation, but the quality issues require follow-up. Procurement will review the corrective-action response with Kestrel’s account manager, Elena Morales, during the September 25 supplier meeting. Receiving will continue 100% inspection of the affected product lines through the end of September, with results reported in the next weekly review.
